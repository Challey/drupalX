# DrupalX Developer Participation Agreement (DPA) v1.0

> Status: **product draft** (not a substitute for counsel-reviewed legal text)  
> Version: 1.0  
> Audience: certified / applying store developers

## 1. Purpose

This agreement governs participation in the DrupalX curated App Store and access to **L2 partner vault** materials (full libraries and deep documentation).

## 2. Eligibility

Developer must complete identity / org verification and Platform review before L2 access and listing privileges.

## 3. Source obligations

1. Submitted apps must provide L3 source to Platform and installing Tenants as required by catalog `source_policy`  
2. Dual-package apps (O1-B): GPL adapter module + DX-RAL business library must be correctly labeled  
3. Developer must not leak L2 vault contents to uncertified third parties

## 4. Security & trust

No malware, undisclosed telemetry, or unauthorized data exfiltration. Security issues must be reported per Platform SLA. Government trust tiers may restrict `community` packages.

## 5. Revenue share

Store pricing and revenue share follow catalog / license records. Platform may withhold payouts for breach or chargebacks.

## 6. Suspension

Platform may suspend listing, L2 credentials, and payouts for DPA or DX-RAL related abuse (including Tenant fourth-party leaks facilitated by Developer).

## 7. Acceptance

Signing this DPA (UI or Drush) records `agreement_id=dpa` and version for the signing user.
