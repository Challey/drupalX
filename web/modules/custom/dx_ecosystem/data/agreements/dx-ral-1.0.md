# DrupalX Reciprocal App License (DX-RAL) v1.0

> Status: **product draft** (not a substitute for counsel-reviewed legal text)  
> Version: 1.0  
> Applies to: store apps marked `license_family=dx_ral` or `dual` (business library portion)

## 1. Parties

- **Platform**: DrupalX operator  
- **Tenant**: the organization or person that lawfully installs / purchases the app  
- **Developer**: the publisher of the app (bound separately by the DPA)

## 2. Grant

Subject to this license, Platform and Developer grant Tenant a non-exclusive right to:

1. Use the app inside the Tenant’s DrupalX tenancy  
2. View and modify source made available under L3 (tenant app source) for the Tenant’s own operations  
3. Receive updates distributed through the curated App Store

## 3. Prohibited fourth-party disclosure

Tenant **must not**:

1. Provide app source, builds, or internal docs to any **fourth party** (anyone other than Platform and Tenant’s authorized personnel)  
2. Publish public mirrors, forks, or redistribution channels outside DrupalX  
3. Re-list / sideload the app outside the curated store  
4. Strip or obscure license / audit markings

## 4. GPL carve-out

If a file is a Drupal derivative and must be GPL-2.0+, GPL governs that file. DX-RAL governs non-derivative business libraries and documentation explicitly labeled DX-RAL.

## 5. Audit & termination

Platform may log source downloads and revoke L3 download / updates for breach. Installed runtime handling is defined by Platform policy (default: continue running, lose updates and L3 access).

## 6. Acceptance

Installing or enabling the app constitutes acceptance of the DX-RAL version shown at install time.
